Bold flavors. Built for the street.

Nova Bites brings Nigerian classics and global street food together — one truck, endless fusion.

From delicious rice and stew to spicy pepper soup and well garnished noodles, we prepare tasty meals that are perfect for students, workers, families and anyone looking for a quick and satisfying meal.

Why Nova Bites?

Locally Sourced Ingredients

We use fresh ingredients sourced from local suppliers and markets. This helps us maintain the taste and quality of every meal we prepare.

Bold Fusion Flavors

Our meals combine traditional Nigerian flavours with creative street-food ideas. Every plate is prepared to give you a familiar taste with something new.

Fast Truck-Side Service

We understand that people are busy. Our food is prepared and served quickly without ignoring the quality and taste of the meal.

Food Made With Purpose

Nova Bites was created to make good food more accessible to people in Abakaliki. We believe that street food should be affordable, enjoyable and prepared with care.


Whether you are grabbing lunch during work, meeting friends after school or simply craving a delicious Nigerian meal, Nova Bites is ready to serve you.
